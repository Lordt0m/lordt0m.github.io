# Orderlane interface

Mode: Operate. A restaurant service counter, with warm cream paper, dark forest ink and orange action controls. The menu and order desk carry the first viewport. No marketing hero. Serif headings pair with quiet sans-serif controls. Food is described through authored menu content, not decorative illustrations. Layout: header and view switch, broad working pane, narrower receipt pane. Mobile stacks the receipt beneath the task.

Tokens: paper #f8f5ed, ink #193d32, secondary #4f655b, line #d8ddd2, orange #b9401b, white #ffffff. Display Georgia; body Segoe UI/system UI. Large status markers and tabular money. Focus outline forest green; native semantic buttons and labelled fields; status output uses polite live announcements. Reduced motion removes receipt entry animation.
