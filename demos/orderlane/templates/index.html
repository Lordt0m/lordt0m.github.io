What is implemented

Order integrity
Server-owned prices, integer naira amounts, validated quantities and a unique request-key constraint.
Safe updates
Explicit status transitions and conditional revision updates. The status and its audit event commit together.
Restaurant boundaries
Session-scoped workspaces and server-filtered restaurant queries. A demo role switcher stands in for login.
Deliberate limits
SQLite on temporary hosting. One application worker. No payment, delivery, production authentication, load-test or PostgreSQL concurrency claims.
